What the Keep is for

Every lesson has a price.
Pay it once.

Units learn the same lessons over and over, because the people who learned them move on. The Keep exists to stop that. It holds the record, turns it into doctrine, and teaches it — so that what one team paid for, the next one inherits.

  1. 01 Debrief It starts with the people it happened to, while it is fresh.
  2. 02 Record Written down with a date, a place and a name attached.
  3. 03 Teach It becomes a course, taught by someone who was there.
  4. 04 Revise And it is argued again the next time reality disagrees.

Method

We change the syllabus
rarely, and never quietly.

01 Taught by practitioners

Nobody teaches a subject here they have not done, and every instructor goes back to the job between cohorts.

02 Written from evidence

Every line in the syllabus traces to a debrief, with a date and a name attached to it.

03 Argued in the open

Changes are defended in front of the people who will have to teach them, or they do not happen.

04 Outlives any posting

Nothing important is allowed to live in one person’s head, or to leave the building with them.

Learn it here.
Or learn it out there.
